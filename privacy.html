Privacy Policy

Asma. Last updated 18 September 2026.

Asma collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising identifiers, and no servers of ours for anything to be sent to.

What stays on your device

Everything you create in Asma is written to your iPhone or iPad and never leaves it except through your own iCloud device backup, if you have one enabled:

  • Journal entries, both handwritten and typed
  • The name you enter, used only for the greeting
  • The feelings you choose and the Circle of Names built from them
  • Dhikr counts, memorisation progress, and reminder settings

Deleting the app deletes all of it. We cannot see any of it, recover it, or restore it for you.

What we collect

Nothing. Asma contains no analytics framework, no crash reporting service, no advertising SDK and no tracking of any kind. We do not know how many people use the app, which Names they read, or what they write.

Purchases

Asma Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment and tells the app only whether a subscription is currently active. We never see your name, your payment details or your Apple ID. Apple's handling of that transaction is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

The daily reminder is scheduled on your device by your device. No notification is sent from a server, and nothing about it is reported anywhere.

Links out of the app

Quran references open quran.com and hadith references open sunnah.com in your browser. Those are separate websites with their own privacy policies. Asma sends them nothing about you beyond the verse or hadith you tapped.

Children

Asma is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will appear at this address with a new date. Since the app collects nothing, any change would be about something newly added, and it would be described plainly here first.
