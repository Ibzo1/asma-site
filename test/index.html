Join the testers

  1. Tap Join the testers below. Google Groups opens.

  4. Come back to this page. Step 2 will be waiting.

Get the app

  1. Tap Become a tester below. Google Play opens.
  2. Tap Become a tester there too.
  3. Tap Download it on Google Play, then Install.
  4. Open Asma. Pro is on for your first two weeks.

If Play says the app isn't available, wait a few minutes and try again.
